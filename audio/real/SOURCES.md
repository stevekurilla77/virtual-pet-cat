# Sunbeam cat and bird sound sources

Real recorded samples collected 2026-11-10 for possible use in the Sunbeam App Store game.

**License policy:** only CC0 / public domain, or CC-BY with simple attribution. CC-BY-SA, GFDL, CC-BY-NC, editorial, AI-generated, synthesized, and human-imitated sounds were rejected. Every keeper license was checked on its source page (Wikimedia Commons API/file page, OpenGameArt license field, or Freesound Creative Commons 0 / `creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/` link).

**Freesound originals:** full WAV/FLAC downloads require a Freesound login. Where noted, the kept original is the public HQ MP3 preview from `cdn.freesound.org` (same recording). License was verified on the public sound page, not inferred from a pack page alone.

**Web encodes:** `/workspace/cat-samples/web/` — mono, 44.1 kHz, 96 kbps MP3, high-pass at 50 Hz, loudness normalization, true-peak limiter, short fades. Total **25 clips, 980,993 bytes (0.94 MB)**.

**Sampler (listen first):** `/workspace/cat-samples/sampler.mp3` (96.1 s, 1,152,984 bytes). Clips play in the numbered order below, with a short gap between clips and a slightly longer gap between categories (meows, purrs, hisses, growls/yowls, birds).

Originals are in `/workspace/cat-samples/raw/`. The GitHub game repo was not modified.

## Quality notes (summary)

- **Best all-around meows:** Kerzoven soft / food / mew+purr, patchytherat meow 2 and whiney meow 2, and the CC-BY British Shorthair hungry calls (clean 48 kHz).
- **Kitten:** Real 8-week kitten; 16 kHz source is a bit dull on top but unmistakably a kitten, not an adult pitched up.
- **Young female and Siamese:** Realistic and distinct voices. Siamese original had slight clipping; limited in the encode. Young female is close and a little compressed-sounding (low crest factor) but not clipped.
- **Curious/touch mew (Tsester):** Short and a bit quieter in character even after normalize; useful as a small reactive sound.
- **IgnasD short meow:** Very brief and clean. The OpenGameArt page does not say it is synthesized (unlike a separate LabChirp upload that was rejected). Sounds like a single real meow; if in doubt, prefer the Kerzoven / patchytherat greetings.
- **Purrs:** Kerzoven sleepy loop is the cleanest and is actually loopable. Whiskers is a genuine 10.7 s continuous purr, slightly noisier, with a brief hot peak. A widely shared "cat purring loop" on Freesound (soundofsong, 650575, CC0) was **not** used in the web set: energy analysis showed intermittent quiet bursts rather than a continuous 10+ s purr.
- **Hisses:** Zabuhailo (especially the 96 kHz Commons original) and the two Sadiquecat close-mic excerpts are the most realistic. A patchytherat hiss was dropped (only ~0.4 s of quiet hiss in an 8 s file).
- **Growl/yowl:** Zabuhailo howl and attack/howl are the most usable angry lines. simon.danielsson growl 3 is a short true growl but was recorded quieter. patchytherat angry excerpt is more distant. Zabuhailo's `catGrowls.wav` was dropped (peak ~0.03, would boost noise badly).
- **Birds:** Short chirps and the warbler tweets are clean enough for window / garden one-shots. The 12 s Abuja garden bed has natural gaps between phrases (real garden, not a dense dawn-chorus wall of sound).
- **Rejected after download (still in `raw/`):**
  - `commons_meow_pleading.oga` and `commons_meow_getting_out_impatient.ogg` (Heismark, public domain) — distinctive pleading/impatient meows but **8 kHz telephone quality**.
  - `commons_meow_notrip.ogg` (Heismark, public domain) — includes the owner's spoken "No".
  - `commons_purr_hauskatze_schnell.ogg` (User:Mattes, public domain) — 17 s, but recorded on a Sony Ericsson phone and very uneven / noisy.
  - `commons_purr_mysid.oga` (Mysid, public domain) — 9.8 s, 22 kHz, peak overshoot; replaced by better purrs.
  - `commons_purr_bertie.ogg` (jim_mowatt via pdsounds.org, public domain) — warm continuous purr but only 8.7 s.
  - `commons_purr_whiskers_edit.ogg` — cleaner but 9.5 s; longer original was preferred.
  - `oga_cat_purractive_loop.wav`, `oga_cat_mewpurr.wav` — extras from the same CC0 pack; sleepy loop and mewpurr2 were preferred.
  - Freesound previews of soundofsong 650575, patchytherat purr/hiss/whiney 1, simon.danielsson meows and growl 5, Zabuhailo growls/howling1, plus extra bird files (Jidanni separated chirps, Australian morning, flock, DCPoke "Birds Singing 03") — license OK (CC0) but weaker quality, redundant, or not a continuous useful texture.
- **Not used at all (license or nature):** Commons and other files under **CC-BY-SA** or **GFDL** (ShareAlike is a poor fit for a commercial App Store game). OpenGameArt "Kitten Mew" by AntumDeluge (human imitation). OpenGameArt LabChirp meow (synthesized). SFXMint "Angry Cat Hiss" (listed as AI-generated). BBC recordings.
